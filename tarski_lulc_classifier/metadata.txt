No Remotior Sensus, no SAGA binaries, no extra installs.
  Supervised: Minimum Distance, Mahalanobis, Maximum Likelihood, Spectral Angle Mapper, Parallelepiped, Random Forest, SVM, Multi-layer Perceptron.
  Unsupervised: K-Means, ISODATA.
  Post-processing: majority filter, sieve, accuracy assessment (confusion matrix, OA, kappa), area report.
  All tools are also available in the Processing Toolbox and Model Builder.
